AI-Driven Automated Crop type, Moisture Stress Detection and irrigation advisory Across Growth Stages Using Moderate Resolution Spectral Signatures (Optical & Microwave Satellite Data)
Accurate, timely identification of crop types and detection of moisture stress across phenological stages are critical for precision agriculture, drought monitoring, yield forecasting, and climate-resilient farm management. Satellite remote sensing—consisting of optical (e.g., LISS-IV. LISS-III, AWiFS, Sentinel-2, Landsat, MODIS etc.,) and microwave SAR (e.g., Sentinel-1, EOS-4, NISAR upcoming etc.,)—provides continuous, large-scale monitoring capabilities. Optical data captures vegetation vigour and biochemical properties, while microwave data penetrates clouds and is sensitive to soil moisture and crop structure.
